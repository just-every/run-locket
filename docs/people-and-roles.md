The person who sets Locket up is its owner. Everyone else is invited and given one role.

## Invite someone

As the owner, open **Activity**, find **Invite a person or agent**, and enter their **Email**, **Name** and **Role**. They receive a sign-in link. Change a role later from **People and access** on the same page; a changed role applies from their next request.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including export, move-in and roles |
| Counsel | The library, legal exceptions, restricted agreements and execution mismatches |
| Approver | Commercial approval, renewal decisions and approving notices for sending |
| Requester | Requests, versions, attachments and the signature handoff |
| Viewer | Reads |
| Agent | Reads and prepares proposals only |

## Restricted agreements

A restricted agreement, and everything attached to it, is visible only to the owner, counsel, its requester, its responsible person and people granted access. To anyone else it does not appear in lists, search or downloads. Grant access on the agreement's **Details and access** tab.

## The activity log

**Activity** records every consequential change: when, who, in which role, and on which agreement. Agents appear by name with the agent role.
