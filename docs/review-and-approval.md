Review checks a version against your clause playbook; approval pins the exact version that will be signed.

## Templates and the clause playbook

Under **Library** the owner or counsel keep **Agreement templates**, with fields and clause wordings, and approve a template version before anyone can use it. A later template version never changes an agreement already made from an earlier one.

The **Clause playbook** holds your rules: preferred wording, allowed alternatives, and who decides an exception (counsel or an approver). One playbook version is active at a time, and every finding records the version it used. Reviews need an active playbook.

## Record findings

On an agreement's **Review** tab, record a finding against a playbook rule:

- **Result**: **Rule met**, **Rule breached** or **Cannot determine**.
- **Exact passage**: the words from the version, checked against its text.
- **Explanation**, **Proposed wording (optional)** and **Open questions (one per line)**.

## Approve the exact version

Every agreement needs commercial approval from the owner or an approver. Each breached or undetermined finding also needs an exception decision from the rule's authority. A requester cannot approve their own request unless they are the owner.

An approval counts only for the version and context it was given on: the version's files and text, the value, the counterparty and the findings that need a decision. A new version or a changed value withdraws earlier approvals, visibly, and the agreement goes back to review. A finding that a rule is met changes nothing an approval rests on.

Locket organises and prepares agreements. It does not give legal advice or decide whether an agreement is valid or enforceable.
